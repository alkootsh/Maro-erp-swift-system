### Key Milestone Deliverables
1. **Q1 2027 (AI Intelligence Layer)**: Gemini AI stock forecasting, OCR invoice scanning to draft purchase bills, Arabic natural language query voice assistant.
2. **Q2 2027 (Mobile First ERP)**: Native cross-platform mobile apps for mobile sales reps and portable Bluetooth ESC/POS receipt printing.
3. **Q3 2027 (Vertical Industry Plugins)**: Launch of Hypermarket, Pharmacy, and Restaurant plugins via the MARO Plugin Engine.
4. **Q4 2027 (E-Commerce Omnichannel)**: Bi-directional synchronization with web storefronts (Shopify, WooCommerce, Custom API).
