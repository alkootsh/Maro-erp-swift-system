# MARO ERP - Universal Print Engine Documentation (v3.0)
## Master Enterprise Protocol v3.0

### Architectural Overview
The MARO Universal Print Engine provides template-driven, high-speed document printing and export capability across all enterprise modules.

### Supported Output Formats & Devices
- **Screen Interactive Preview**: Vector-rendered preview with print zoom and page selection.
- **PDF Export**: High-resolution vectorized PDF output.
- **Excel & CSV Export**: Structured tabular exports for financial and inventory reports.
- **Paper Sizes & Thermal Printers**:
  - Standard A4 (210mm x 297mm)
  - Standard A5 (148mm x 210mm)
  - 80mm Thermal Receipts (POS / Kitchen / Cashier)
  - 58mm Compact Thermal Receipts

### Template Features
- Dynamic Header / Footer with Company Logo, Tax ID, Branch Details, and QR Code (ZATCA / ETA compliant).
- Barcode Rendering (Code128, EAN13, QR Code) for Invoice Verification & Serial Tracking.
- Full RTL (Arabic) & LTR (English) bilingual rendering.

### Supported Document Types
- Sales Invoices & Return Receipts
- Purchase Invoices & Receiving Vouchers
- Quotations & Sales Orders
- Shift Closing & Cash Drawer Reconciliation Reports
- Customer & Supplier Statements of Account
- Stock Cards & Inventory Movement Sheets
- Financial Reports (P&L, Balance Sheet, VAT Return, Trial Balance)
