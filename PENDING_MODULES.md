# MARO Business Platform - Pending Modules

The following enterprise modules are scheduled for implementation starting in Sprint 8 following approval of the Sprint 8 Architecture Design:

## Sprint 8: Enterprise Sales, POS, Procurement & Multi-Channel Logistics
- **Offline Touch & Mobile POS Engine**: High-frequency sales terminal supporting barcode scanners, weight scales, offline receipt printing, and split payments.
- **Enterprise Sales & Invoicing**: Retail, Wholesale, Hypermarket, Quotations, Pro-forma, Sales Orders, Delivery Notes, and Credit Notes.
- **Procurement & Supplier Payable Engine**: Requisitions, PO approval workflows, Goods Received Notes (GRN), and supplier bills.
- **Multi-Branch & Multi-Warehouse Logistics**: Inter-branch transfer requests, batch/serial tracking, expiry management, and price list matrix.

## Sprint 9: Financial Accounting & General Ledger (GL)
- **Chart of Accounts (COA)**: Hierarchical 5-level account tree, automatic double-entry journal postings for sales, purchases, and inventory movements.
- **Financial Statements**: Real-time Trial Balance, Income Statement (P&L), Balance Sheet, Statement of Cash Flows.

## Sprint 10: Human Resources, Payroll & Plugin Ecosystem
- **Employee Master & Attendance**: Biometric device logs, shift schedules, leave management.
- **Payroll Processor**: Salary structure calculation, tax deductions, social security, automated payslip generation.
- **Plugin Infrastructure**: Plug-and-play architecture for Vertical Solutions (Retail, Restaurant, Pharmacy, Manufacturing).
